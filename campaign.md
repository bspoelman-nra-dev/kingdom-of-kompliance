# The Kingdom of Kompliance

**A D&D 5e Campaign for National Restaurant Association Employees**

## Campaign Overview

**Hook**: In the Kingdom of Kompliance, the ancient Guild of Hospitality has splintered into warring factions. The Council of Codes (health inspectors turned tyrants) has seized power, imposing impossible regulations that threaten to shut down every tavern, inn, and food hall in the realm. Meanwhile, the Franchise Kingdoms expand their territories, the Independent Innkeepers resist, and the mysterious Lobbyist's Circle pulls strings from the shadows. Heroes must navigate this bureaucratic nightmare to restore balance before the kingdom's hospitality industry collapses entirely.

**Setting**: The Kingdom of Kompliance - a fantasy realm where taverns are temples, menus are magical contracts, and health codes have the force of divine law.

**Level Range**: 3-10 (suitable for intermediate players)

**Themes**: Regulatory absurdity, industry politics, worker solidarity, franchise vs. independent dynamics, compliance culture

---

## Act 1: The Inspection Inquisition (Levels 3-5)

### Chapter Summary
The party discovers that health inspectors have gained supernatural powers through the "Codex of Compliance" - an ancient artifact that allows them to enforce regulations with magical authority. Small taverns and inns are being shut down overnight, their owners imprisoned in the "Citation Dungeons." The heroes must investigate this corruption while helping local establishments survive impossible inspections.

### Possible Quests

**Quest 1: The Impossible Audit**
- **Objective**: Help Grimble's Grub Hub pass a surprise inspection with 347 new regulations implemented overnight
- **Challenges**: Fetch magical ingredients, install mystical ventilation systems, defeat summoned "Violation Wraiths"
- **Reward**: 500 GP, Grimble's eternal gratitude, "Badge of Barely Compliant" (magic item)

**Quest 2: Rescue from Citation Dungeon**
- **Objective**: Break out wrongly imprisoned tavern owners from the Citation Dungeon
- **Challenges**: Navigate bureaucratic traps, solve regulation riddles, face the Warden of Violations
- **Reward**: 750 GP, freed innkeepers provide safe houses throughout the kingdom

**Quest 3: The Codex Conspiracy**
- **Objective**: Investigate how the Council of Codes obtained the Codex of Compliance
- **Challenges**: Infiltrate the Bureau of Standards, discover the Lobbyist's Circle involvement
- **Reward**: 1000 GP, knowledge of Act 2 antagonists, ally in the Council

**Quest 4: Unite the Independents**
- **Objective**: Convince scattered independent tavern owners to form a resistance guild
- **Challenges**: Travel between hidden establishments, negotiate with rival owners, defend a secret meeting
- **Reward**: Access to Independent Innkeepers' Guild resources and network

### Key Locations
- **Grimble's Grub Hub**: Struggling halfling tavern, party's first mission
- **Citation Dungeon**: Magical prison beneath the Bureau of Standards
- **The Smoky Backroom**: Secret meeting place for independent operators
- **Bureau of Standards**: Fortress-like government building, heavily guarded

### Key NPCs

**Inspector Hardcastle**
- **Role**: Primary antagonist of Act 1
- **Class/Level**: Cleric (Domain of Order) Level 7
- **Details**: Former reasonable inspector corrupted by the Codex's power; genuinely believes extreme enforcement protects citizens; wears pristine white robes with dozens of citation seals; can magically "mark" establishments for closure
- **Connection**: Unwitting pawn of the Lobbyist's Circle, redeemable if shown the conspiracy

**Grimble Stonefoot**
- **Role**: Quest giver and ally
- **Class/Level**: Halfling Commoner (former Cook - Expert NPC)
- **Details**: Third-generation tavern owner, kind but exhausted; makes the best mushroom stew in the kingdom; ready to lose everything; represents struggling small businesses
- **Connection**: Becomes key member of Independent Innkeepers' Guild

**Silviana Quickquill**
- **Role**: Mysterious informant
- **Class/Level**: Rogue (Inquisitive) Level 5
- **Details**: Half-elf investigative journalist for "The Daily Digest"; knows too much about the Codex conspiracy; sardonic sense of humor; always scribbling notes
- **Connection**: Reveals Lobbyist's Circle involvement, becomes recurring ally

## Act 2: The Franchise Wars (Levels 6-8)

### Chapter Summary
While the party dealt with the Council of Codes, the three great Franchise Kingdoms seized the opportunity to expand aggressively. The Golden Arches Kingdom, the Burger Crown Empire, and the Pizza Dominion are buying up territories, hiring mercenaries, and crushing independents. The party must choose sides, forge alliances, or find a third path while uncovering who's funding this expansion.

### Possible Quests

**Quest 1: The Hostile Takeover**
- **Objective**: Defend a beloved independent inn from forced acquisition by a Franchise Kingdom
- **Challenges**: Legal battles (persuasion/investigation checks), economic sabotage investigation, potential combat with franchise enforcers
- **Reward**: 1200 GP, magic item from grateful inn owner, reputation with independents

**Quest 2: Inside the Golden Arches**
- **Objective**: Infiltrate the Golden Arches Kingdom to learn their expansion strategy
- **Challenges**: Pose as franchise trainees, navigate corporate hierarchy, steal strategic plans
- **Reward**: 1500 GP, intel on Act 3 villain, "Uniform of Universal Acceptance" (disguise magic item)

**Quest 3: The Worker's Uprising**
- **Objective**: Help franchise workers organize for better conditions
- **Challenges**: Protect union organizers, negotiate with franchise lords, handle corporate countermeasures
- **Reward**: 1500 GP, worker network provides information and assistance

**Quest 4: The Secret Menu**
- **Objective**: Discover why all three franchise kingdoms are seeking an ancient recipe
- **Challenges**: Dungeon crawl through abandoned legendary restaurant, solve culinary puzzles, face recipe guardian
- **Reward**: 2000 GP, legendary recipe (key to Act 3), powerful consumable magic items

### Key Locations
- **Castle Golden Arches**: Sprawling franchise headquarters with military precision
- **The Contested Territory**: Border region where all three franchises compete
- **The Last Independent Square**: District of holdout small businesses
- **Vault of Recipes Past**: Ancient dungeon containing legendary culinary secrets

### Key NPCs

**Lord Ronald the Franchiser**
- **Role**: Golden Arches Kingdom ruler, secondary antagonist
- **Class/Level**: Warlock (Pact of the Chain - Corporate Sponsor) Level 10
- **Details**: Charismatic human noble obsessed with "efficiency" and "scalability"; genuinely believes franchising helps everyone; wears golden armor; commands chain devil familiar named "Synergy"
- **Connection**: Funded by the Lobbyist's Circle, competing for the legendary recipe

**Margot the Organizer**
- **Role**: Labor leader and ally
- **Class/Level**: Bard (College of Eloquence) Level 6
- **Details**: Passionate dwarf who worked in franchise kitchens for 20 years; inspiring speaker; carries a rolling pin as weapon; knows every worker's struggle
- **Connection**: Can rally workers to party's cause, provides vital intelligence

**The Tip Phantom**
- **Role**: Mysterious benefactor
- **Class/Level**: Unknown (appears as hooded figure)
- **Details**: Anonymously leaves large tips at struggling establishments; seems to predict party's movements; identity revealed in Act 3
- **Connection**: Actually an undercover member of the original Guild of Hospitality

## Act 3: The Lobbyist's Gambit (Levels 9-10)

### Chapter Summary
All threads converge: the Lobbyist's Circle manipulated everything. They corrupted the Council of Codes to destroy independents, funded the Franchise Wars to consolidate power, and seek the legendary recipe to create a monopoly artifact. The true villain - Maximilian Margin, the ancient lich who leads the Lobbyist's Circle - plans to use the "Recipe of Ultimate Compliance" to magically bind every eating establishment in the kingdom to his will. The party must unite former enemies and storm the Circle's hidden sanctum.

### Possible Quests

**Quest 1: Forging the Alliance**
- **Objective**: Unite independents, reformed inspectors, workers, and reasonable franchise leaders
- **Challenges**: Negotiate peace between bitter rivals, prove the common threat, defend alliance summit from assassins
- **Reward**: 2500 GP, command of united forces, legendary magic items from each faction

**Quest 2: The Sanctum Assault**
- **Objective**: Infiltrate the Lobbyist's Circle sanctum beneath the capital
- **Challenges**: Navigate lobbying traps ("Persuasion Mazes"), defeat corrupted former heroes, overcome magical contracts
- **Reward**: 3000 GP, chance to stop the ritual

**Quest 3: The Recipe Heist**
- **Objective**: Steal the Recipe of Ultimate Compliance before Maximilian completes it
- **Challenges**: Time-pressure heist mission, elite guardian encounters, moral choice about destroying vs. preserving the recipe
- **Reward**: 3500 GP, fate of the kingdom

**Quest 4: The Final Feast**
- **Objective**: Confront Maximilian Margin and stop his scheme
- **Challenges**: Epic boss battle against lich and his inner circle, break magical contracts binding the kingdom
- **Reward**: 5000 GP, kingdom's gratitude, establishment of new balanced Guild of Hospitality

### Key Locations
- **The Silver Spoon Club**: Luxurious club concealing entrance to Circle's sanctum
- **The Contract Vaults**: Labyrinthine archive of every magical agreement ever signed
- **The Kitchen Eternal**: Maximilian's ritual chamber, a vast supernatural kitchen
- **Parliament of Plates**: Capital building where final confrontation can be made public

### Key NPCs

**Maximilian Margin**
- **Role**: Primary campaign villain
- **Class/Level**: Lich (Modified), effective Level 14
- **Details**: Ancient restaurateur who became obsessed with controlling the entire industry; transformed himself into a lich to achieve eternal influence; wears fine suit made of interwoven contracts; phylactery is hidden in first franchise agreement ever signed; eloquent and believes he's saving the kingdom from "chaos"
- **Connection**: Mastermind behind everything, final boss

**The Tip Phantom (Revealed: Maestro Benevolence)**
- **Role**: Secret ally, founder of original Guild
- **Class/Level**: Monk (Way of Mercy) Level 12
- **Details**: Last surviving founder of the Guild of Hospitality; went underground when Maximilian seized control; has been anonymously supporting resistance; elderly human with kitchen-scarred hands and warm eyes
- **Connection**: Provides key intelligence, can aid in final battle, helps rebuild afterward

**Inspector Hardcastle (Redeemed)**
- **Role**: Reformed antagonist turned ally
- **Class/Level**: Cleric Level 9 (renounced corruption, gained new abilities)
- **Details**: Broke free from Codex's control after party exposed conspiracy; wracked with guilt but determined to make amends; brings insider knowledge of government systems
- **Connection**: Can turn Council of Codes agents, knows sanctum's defenses

**Silviana Quickquill**
- **Role**: Chronicler and ally
- **Class/Level**: Rogue Level 9
- **Details**: Has been documenting everything for explosive exposé; will ensure truth is known regardless of outcome; proves corporate funding of entire conspiracy
- **Connection**: Her evidence is crucial for final confrontation and political victory

## Campaign Themes & Humor

### Satirical Elements
- **Regulations as Magic**: Health codes become literal spells ("Zone of Proper Handwashing")
- **Franchise Rivalries**: Played as kingdom warfare with corporate language
- **Labor Issues**: Worker struggles presented as heroic resistance movement
- **Lobbying**: Shown as actual dark magic and conspiracy
- **Compliance Culture**: Bureaucratic absurdity taken to fantasy extremes

### Keeping it Fun
- NPCs with terrible restaurant puns for names
- Magic items based on kitchen equipment ("Spatula of Returning", "Apron of Infinite Pockets")
- Combat encounters themed around service industry chaos
- Puzzles involving recipes, menus, and food preparation
- Restaurant inspection mechanics as skill challenges

### Player Engagement Tips
- Let players run their own tavern as downtime activity
- Include real-world frustrations in satirical way (tip culture, supply chains, difficult customers)
- Reward creative solutions that unite rather than divide factions
- Make regulatory challenges feel absurd but recognizable
- End campaign with party establishing new, balanced guild system

---

## Conclusion

The Kingdom of Kompliance campaign allows NRA employees to laugh at industry challenges while playing heroes in a fantasy world. The satirical elements should feel familiar enough to be funny but exaggerated enough to maintain escapism. The ultimate message: cooperation and balance serve the kingdom better than consolidation or chaos.

**Happy adventuring in the Kingdom of Kompliance!**
